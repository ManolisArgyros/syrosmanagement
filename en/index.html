Why a Syros property earns all year round

Ermoupoli, the capital of the Cyclades, is alive in winter too. Besides the summer visitors, professionals, shipyard technicians and civil servants need housing for weeks or months. That is why we plan every property for twelve months, not three.

Frequently asked questions about property management and leasing on Syros

How much does property management on Syros cost?

The fee is a percentage of revenue. You see it written in the business plan, alongside your net income, before you decide.

What does the free business plan include?

A monthly revenue estimate, expenses, taxes and net result for your own property. It is free and you keep it.

How much rent do you pay if I lease you my home?

It depends on location, size and condition. After the visit we give you a written offer with a specific amount, within 7 days.

What applies under Law 5170/2025 and the AMA number?

Since 1 October 2025, short-term rental has stricter requirements, inspections and fines of up to €20,000. We check your property and, if needed, handle the procedure ourselves.

Which areas of Syros do you cover?

The whole island: Ermoupoli, Ano Syros, Foinikas, Posidonia, Galissas, Kini, Vari, Azolimnos and Megas Gialos.

Can I use my home?

With management, yes: you book your dates in advance. With a lease, days for you are agreed in the contract.
